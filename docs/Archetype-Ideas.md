# New archetype ideas

A survey of what the installed mods offer that no skill tree uses yet, and archetypes that could be built from it.
No generic "Sorcerer" (a do-everything arcane caster) is proposed: each idea below has a play style the current
17 archetypes don't cover.

## What is unused

Counted from the installed jars against every reward in the 17 skill trees (the Classes tab included).

| Source | In the pack | Used by a tree | Unused |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Iron's Spells + T.O Magic 'n Extras spells | 184 | 112 | 72 |
| Epic Fight skills you can learn (not weapon-bound) | about 80 | 54 | about 25 |
| Cursed Fate innate techniques | 14 | 2 (Shrine, part of Boogie Woogie) + 2 addons | 11 |

Biggest gaps:

- **Eldritch school**: 12 of 16 spells unused (Eldritch Blast, Sonic Boom, Sculk Tentacles, Telekinesis, Abyssal
  Shroud, Pocket Dimension, Psychic Bolt, Abyssal Blast, Void Devourer, Shadowed Miasma, The Forgotten Beast,
  Reversal). Only Blackout, Spectral Blink and Planar Sight are used.
- **Ender school**: 20 of 27 unused (Starfall, Black Hole, Gravity Fissure, Magic Missile, Magic Arrow, Dragon's
  Breath, Arcane Shackle, Shadow Slash, Summon Swords, Portal, Recall, Summon Ender Chest, Supernova, Void
  Eruption, Shear of the Stars, Eternal Sentinel, Orbital Void, Conjure Void Tomes, Vortex Uppercut, Cursed
  Minefield).
- **T.O's tech spells**: Magnetron Deployment, Death Laser, Rapid Laser, Mechanized Predators, EM Pulse.
- **Melee spells**: Flaming Strike, Spectral Hammer, Gyro Slash, Ignited Onslaught, Burning Judgment, Tectonic
  Rift, Sword of the Ancients, Fang Swirl, Fang Ward, Earthshatter.
- **Summons**: Primal Pack, Summon Desert Dwellers, Primordial Steed, Sticky Steed Summon, Nocturnal Swarm, Call
  Forth the Dead King, Eternal Sentinel.
- **Epic Fight (Weapons of Miracles)**:
  - Guard skills: Counter Attack, Perfect Bulwark, Vengeful Parry.
  - Passives and identities: Mindset, Meditation, Inner Growth, Heart Shield, Unbreakable, Shulker Cloak, Latent
    Retribution, Proximity Exploiter, Arrow Tenacity, Lunatic Vivacity, Time Travel, Back and Forth.
  - Movement: Precise Roll, Ravager Force (charge).
- **Epic Fight (others)**:
  - *Iron's Spells bridge*: **Magic Blade** (casts your selected spell at the end of each swing) and Connect to the
    Root.
  - *Sword Soaring*: flying on your sword, plus its sword-array skills.
  - *Nightfall*: soul-like dodge and step.
- **Cursed Fate innate techniques**:
  - Limitless: Lapse Blue, Reversal Red, Hollow Purple, Infinity.
  - Star Rage: Garuda, Jupiter, Saturn, Supernova, Event Horizon.
  - Disaster Flames: Ember Insects, Lava Palm, Molten Ground, Eruption, Coffin of the Iron Mountain.
  - Blood Manipulation: Piercing Blood, Flowing Red Scale, Convergence, Blood Meteorite.
  - Puppet Manipulation: Ultra Cannon, Ultra Shield, Ultra Spin.
  - Private Pure Love Train: Pachinko Ball, Idle Death Gamble.
  - Cursed Partners: Rika, Copy.
  - Vessel and Heavenly Restriction: Divergent Fist, Heavy Blow, Barrage, Black Flash.
  - Idle Transfiguration, Soul Shrine, Calamitous Shrine.
  - General: Reverse Curse Technique (self-heal), Simple Domain, Veil.

Not counted as usable: weapon-bound skills (Epic Fight's Battojutsu, Guillotine and the like; Weapons of Miracles'
Sakura State, Solar Arcano, Demonic Ascension and the like; Nightfall's Murasama and Yamato skills). These come
with their weapon and can't be tree rewards.

## Proposed archetypes

Ranked by how much new play each adds and how much unused content backs it.

### 1. Warlock (Mage): a pact caster who pays in health

Warlock tops Primagames' list of the most popular D&D classes. Here it uses the almost untouched Eldritch school
and is not a general caster: its spells cost health as well as mana, and it hits harder the lower its health.

- **Spells**: Eldritch Blast, Psychic Bolt, Sonic Boom, Sculk Tentacles, Abyssal Shroud, Shadowed Miasma, Void
  Devourer, Abyssal Blast, and The Forgotten Beast (capstone).
- **Epic Fight**: Connect to the Root, and Latent Retribution.
- **Stats**: eldritch spell power, spell resistance, and a new "pact" attribute (bonus damage at low health).
- **Partners**: Cursed (Hexblade), Necromancy, Phantom.

### 2. Spellblade (bridges Warrior and Mage): weapon swings that cast

The gap between Samurai and the elemental mages. Its centrepiece is **Magic Blade**, already in the pack, which
casts your selected spell at the end of every swing. The Bladesinger and Magus are well-loved versions of this.

- **Spells**: Summon Swords, Shadow Slash, Flaming Strike, Spectral Hammer, Sword of the Ancients, Echoing
  Strikes, Gyro Slash, and Arcane Shackle.
- **Epic Fight**: Magic Blade, Combat Mastery, and Counter Attack.
- **Stats**: a sword or longsword damage bonus, and spell power that also adds to melee damage.
- **Partners**: it pairs with all six elements, e.g. Flame + Spellblade = "Flame Blade".

### 3. Monk (Warrior): unarmed martial artist

Way of the Open Hand is the 6th most-played subclass in Baldur's Gate 3 (The Mary Sue). No archetype here fights
empty-handed, though Shooting Style already gives kicks.

- **Cursed Fate**: Heavy Blow, Barrage, Uppercut, Follow Up Punch, Divergent Fist, Black Flash, and Reverse Curse
  Technique.
- **Epic Fight**: Meditation, Inner Growth, Mindset, Precise Roll, and Shooting Style.
- **Stats**: unarmed damage, attack speed, and stun armour.
- **Partners**: Phantom (kicks), Holy (Warrior Monk), Lightning.

### 4. Astromancer (Mage): star and gravity magic

It suits the pack's Astrologer look. Iron's Starfall plus Cursed Fate's whole **Star Rage** kit give it a full
set of big, slow cosmic spells.

- **Cursed Fate**: Garuda, Jupiter, Saturn, Supernova, Event Horizon.
- **Spells**: Starfall, Shear of the Stars, Orbital Void, Supernova, Black Hole, Gravity Fissure, and Solar Flare.
- **Stats**: ender spell power, cooldown reduction, and cast-time reduction.
- **Partners**: Holy, Ice, Thief.

### 5. Artificer (Ranger): turrets, lasers and constructs

D&D's Artificer is the "builder". It uses T.O's tech spells and Cursed Fate's **Puppet Manipulation**, and fits
alongside Siege Machines.

- **Spells**: Magnetron Deployment, Mechanized Predators, Rapid Laser, Death Laser, EM Pulse, and Eternal Sentinel.
- **Cursed Fate**: Ultra Cannon, Ultra Shield, Ultra Spin.
- **Epic Fight**: Shulker Cloak and Arrow Tenacity.
- **Stats**: summon damage, lightning spell power, and armour.
- **Partners**: Lightning, Stalker, Knight.

### 6. Beastmaster (Ranger): a pack of animal summons

Beast Master is the 8th most-played subclass in Baldur's Gate 3. Wanderer has a horse and a polar bear, but no
one commands a pack. Domestication Innovation is already installed for pets.

- **Spells**: Primal Pack, Summon Desert Dwellers, Primordial Steed, Nocturnal Swarm, and Sticky Steed Summon.
- **Epic Fight**: Proximity Exploiter.
- **Stats**: tamed-creature damage and resistance (the attributes exist), summon damage, and pet health.
- **Overlap**: Wanderer's Summon Horse and Polar Bear could move here.
- **Partners**: Druid, Wanderer, Stalker.

### 7. Sentinel (Warrior): a counter-attack tank

Knight blocks, but nobody **punishes** blocks. It uses the unused Weapons of Miracles guard skills.

- **Epic Fight**: Counter Attack, Perfect Bulwark, Vengeful Parry, Unbreakable, Heart Shield, and Ravager Force.
- **Spells**: Fang Ward, Fang Swirl, Earthshatter, and Tectonic Rift.
- **Stats**: damage reflection, stun armour, and knockback resistance.
- **Partners**: Knight (Bastion), Samurai, Berzerker.

### 8. Gambler (Rogue): luck and risk

It uses Cursed Fate's **Private Pure Love Train** (Hakari's technique). Random rewards make it a fun, high-swing
class.

- **Cursed Fate**: Pachinko Ball and Idle Death Gamble (a jackpot that heals you constantly).
- **Spells**: Firecracker or Lob Creeper. These are Thief's now; it would need new ones, such as Cursed Minefield.
- **Epic Fight**: Lunatic Vivacity, plus the dodge and parry reward perks as random buffs.
- **Stats**: luck, critical chance, and plunder.
- **Partners**: Thief, Phantom, Cursed.

### Smaller ideas, or alternatives to the above

- **Hemomancer** (Rogue or Mage): Cursed Fate Blood Manipulation (Piercing Blood, Flowing Red Scale, Blood
  Meteorite), Vigor Siphon and Eek. It overlaps Necromancy's blood spells, so it would need to be a melee and
  self-buff take.
- **Volcanist** (Mage): Cursed Fate Disaster Flames (Ember Insects, Lava Palm, Molten Ground, Eruption, Coffin of
  the Iron Mountain) with Tectonic Rift, Ignited Onslaught and Extinction. This is the most Flame-like idea; it
  could instead become a second Flame branch.
- **Gravity or Infinity** (Mage): Cursed Fate Limitless (Lapse Blue, Reversal Red, Hollow Purple, Infinity), Black
  Hole, Gravity Fissure and Telekinesis. Strong but very anime-specific; it could merge into Astromancer.
- **Sword Immortal** (Warrior): Sword Soaring (flying on your sword, Celestial Array, Babylonian Armory, Voidblade
  Domain), Summon Swords and Myriad Blades. Samurai already has Myriad Blades.
- **Chronomancer**: Time Travel, Recall, Portal, Reversal, Slow and Haste. Thin, and Slow and Haste are taken.

## More customisation in the existing trees

These need no new archetype:

- **Weapons of Miracles passives as nodes**: Mindset, Heart Shield, Latent Retribution and Precise Roll would give
  Knight, Berzerker and Assassin alternatives to the shared passives (Stamina Pillager is in five trees,
  Swordmaster in four).
- **Second dodges**: Nightfall's soul-like dodge and step, and Precise Roll, so not everyone uses the same roll.
- **Dodge and parry rewards**: Instant Heal, Damage Absorption, Resistance, Speed and Stamina Boost as small
  nodes in Samurai, Phantom and Knight. Only Strength I is used now.
- **Unused spells that fit existing trees**:
  - *Mages*: Flaming Barrage, Burning Judgment and Extinction (Flame); Ascension and Thunderstep (Lightning); Ice
    Tomb (Ice); Echo of the Abyss (Water).
  - *Holy*: Dawn's Favor and Nullflare.
  - *Necromancy*: Call Forth the Dead King.
  - *Others*: Arcane Shackle (Thief); Recall and Portal (Scout); Dragon's Breath (Cursed).

## Practical notes

- Each new archetype needs:
  - its own tree, an icon, a place on the Classes tab, and a level curve;
  - 17 new class-combination names, one per existing archetype (a list to pick from, as before);
  - an entry in the Binder's archetype list, used by the Status, Respec and class names.
- Classes tab: Warrior has 3 archetypes, Mage 6, Rogue 4 and Ranger 4. The proposals above balance this out:
  - Monk and Sentinel go under Warrior.
  - Warlock and Astromancer go under Mage.
  - Gambler goes under Rogue.
  - Artificer and Beastmaster go under Ranger.
  - Spellblade sits between Warrior and Mage.
- Cursed Fate techniques run through the same route as Shrine and Blitz (the Binder's `Technique` list).
  Techniques from another innate kit may check the player's chosen technique, so each one needs testing in game.
  Projection Sorcery and Black Bird already work this way.
- Sword Soaring's skills and some Weapons of Miracles identities need checking in game, to confirm they can be
  learned without their mod's own weapon.
